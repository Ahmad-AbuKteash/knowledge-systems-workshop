# Structure: restore meaningful organization

## Prerequisite
clean complete.

## Concept
Structure expresses relationships between sections, definitions, lists, and tables.

## Procedure
Follow WORKSHOP_MODE.md. Inspect cleaned text. Repair obvious heading hierarchy,
list boundaries, and table structure using original evidence. Keep units and
qualifications attached. Do not manufacture headings or table cells unsupported
by the source. Mark ambiguous extraction for review instead of guessing.
Write structured/documents.json and Markdown copies. Preserve record identity,
source hashes, and the relationship to any excluded duplicates. Show a section
before/after and explain how its structure will help retrieval. Update progress.

## Output
structured/documents.json; Markdown copies; structure report.

## Participant checkpoint
Can the participant follow section boundaries? Does the structure preserve the
original relationships, especially table columns and exceptions?
