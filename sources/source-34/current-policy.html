Nimbus workspace exit, export, and deletion

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: Customer Data Operations.

Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

Applicability

Customer-requested Nimbus workspace closure; individual privacy requests follow source-19.

Export before closure

A verified customer administrator may request a final export while the workspace is active. Customer Data Operations supplies a download available for seven calendar days after completion. This managed exit export is distinct from the 24-hour API export link in source-31. Confirm the export is downloaded before scheduling closure.

Deletion timeline

After a verified closure request, the workspace enters a 14-calendar-day recovery window with normal interactive access disabled. The verified administrator can withdraw the request during that window. Active-system deletion completes within 30 calendar days after the recovery window ends.

Backup aging and holds

Deleted data may remain in isolated rolling backups for up to 35 additional calendar days after active-system deletion under source-27. It is not available for ordinary customer access. A valid scoped legal hold can suspend deletion of matching records; it does not authorize indefinite retention of all unrelated data.

Evidence and boundaries

Record request verification, export delivery, recovery-window dates, active deletion completion, and relevant hold decisions. A subscription cancellation alone does not initiate this workflow. This policy does not promise immediate erasure from every backup or define jurisdiction-specific legal requirements.
