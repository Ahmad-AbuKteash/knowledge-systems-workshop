Nimbus partner demonstrations and public claims

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: Partner Enablement.

Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

Applicability

Nimbus demonstrations, partner training, and externally shared product claims; executed commercial terms require source-20.

Demo preparation

Use a dedicated sandbox with synthetic records. Check the reset calendar in source-32 and prepare a recorded fallback that contains no customer data. Demonstrators must verify which features are generally available, preview-only, or simulated before presenting them.

Claims and approvals

Published capability claims need product-owner approval and supporting documentation. Quantified performance claims need reproducible measurement context. Preview dates must be identified as non-binding plans unless a separately authorized contract states otherwise.

Recording and distribution

Obtain participant agreement before recording and state the intended audience. Review recordings for exposed keys, customer data, and internal-only discussions before sharing. A partner's request does not authorize disclosure of restricted material.

Commercial boundaries

Demo accounts cannot process real customer payments. A slide, sales email, or oral promise does not establish an SLA, refund exception, or discount. Route proposed contractual commitments to Legal and the authorized signatory. Support can clarify existing published behavior but cannot approve a new product guarantee.
