Nimbus contractual service credit requests

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: Customer Success Operations.

Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

Applicability

Nimbus Managed Services accounts with a signed credit-bearing SLA; Nimbus Core has no credit entitlement under this document.

Eligibility

Customers must submit a credit request within 15 calendar days after the affected calendar month ends. Include account ID, incident references, affected service, and the signed SLA reference. Customer Success verifies contractual scope before calculating a credit.

Illustrative contract schedule

For Managed Services contracts explicitly incorporating schedule NIM-SLA-2026B, monthly availability below 99.9% and at least 99.0% yields a 5% credit; below 99.0% yields 10%. Availability of exactly 99.9% earns no credit. The signed agreement defines the measurement and exclusions.

Calculation and treatment

Credit = eligible monthly recurring service fee x applicable percentage, capped at 10% of that fee for the month. Taxes and setup charges are excluded. Apply an approved credit to a future invoice; it is not a cash refund. Do not add multiple incident credits beyond the monthly cap.

Boundaries

An internal SLO breach under source-25 does not establish contractual availability. Refund requests follow the offering-specific policies in source-03 or source-06. This document does not prove that any particular account incorporated the illustrated schedule.
