Nimbus invoice matching and payment changes

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: Accounts Payable.

Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

Applicability

Supplier invoices payable by Nimbus; customer subscription charges are outside this procedure.

Invoice intake

Send invoices through the approved Accounts Payable portal with supplier identity, invoice number, purchase-order reference, currency, tax, and service period. The budget owner confirms receipt of goods or services. Accounts Payable matches supplier, purchase order, and receipt before scheduling payment.

Payment timing

Standard terms are 30 calendar days from receipt of a valid, matched invoice unless the signed contract states otherwise. The receipt timestamp of an incomplete invoice does not start that clock. Record the reason and notify the supplier when returning an invoice.

Bank detail changes

A supplier bank-account change requires independent verification through a previously verified contact method and approval by a second Accounts Payable reviewer. An email reply or invoice footer alone is not verification. Hold payment until verification completes.

Duplicate checks

Match supplier identity, invoice number, amount, and service period. A corrected invoice must refer to the original and be marked as replacement or credit. Similar amounts alone do not establish duplication. Procurement rules are in source-14; customer duplicate-charge refunds are in source-06.
