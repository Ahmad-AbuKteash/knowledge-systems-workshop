# Nimbus customer billing and plan changes

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: Billing Operations.
Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

## Applicability
Nimbus Core subscriptions purchased directly from Nimbus; Nimbus Managed Services refunds use source-06.

## Plans and billing
Nimbus Core offers monthly and annual prepaid terms. An upgrade takes effect immediately after an authorized administrator confirms the displayed quote. A downgrade takes effect at the next renewal and does not generate an automatic refund for the current term.

## Proration
For an upgrade, incremental charge = (new daily price - old daily price) x remaining full days. Daily price is the applicable term price divided by its actual number of calendar days. Exclude the change day and round the final charge once to cents. The quote must show taxes separately.

## Cancellation
Cancellation stops renewal; it does not immediately delete the workspace. The paid term continues unless an approved refund changes its end date. Nimbus Core first-payment refund eligibility is governed by source-03, not the Managed Services window.

## Disputes and boundaries
Billing Operations reviews calculation disputes against the accepted quote and invoice. A sales conversation is not an accepted price change. This guide does not authorize backdated discounts, state specific plan prices, or define customer data export windows; see source-34 for workspace exit.
