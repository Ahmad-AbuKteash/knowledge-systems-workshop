# Billing clarification: Core versus Managed Services

Record version: 1.0. Owner: Billing Operations. Access scope: workshop-public.
Record type: notice.

Published: 2026-10-02. Effective: 2026-09-01 onward.
Nimbus sells two distinct offerings. Nimbus Core is the self-service subscription governed by source-03/refunds-current.html: its 30-calendar-day first-payment window does not cover renewal payments. Nimbus Managed Services is a separately contracted managed offering governed by source-06/current-policy.pdf, with different purchase-type windows and a duplicate-charge exception. Identify the invoice offering before applying a policy. A customer's company name or the word "subscription" alone does not select the policy. This clarification does not change either offering's eligibility rules.
