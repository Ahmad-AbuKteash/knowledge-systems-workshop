Nimbus purchasing and supplier onboarding

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: Procurement.

Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

Applicability

New Nimbus purchases of goods and services, excluding employee travel reimbursement and payroll.

Approval thresholds

Total committed value in USDApproval before commitment
Up to and including 1,000Budget owner
Above 1,000 through 10,000Budget owner and Procurement
Above 10,000Budget owner, Procurement, and Finance director

Calculating value

Use the full non-cancellable contract term, including setup and mandatory fees, before tax. Convert foreign currency using the Finance rate recorded in the request. Splitting one commitment into smaller purchase orders does not change the threshold.

Supplier checks

Any supplier receiving Nimbus customer data needs Security review and a signed data-processing agreement before transfer, regardless of price. Procurement confirms vendor identity and tax details. Approval to buy is not permission to sign: signature authority follows source-20.

Urgent requests

An incident commander may request expedited review for service restoration. The same approvers must record their decisions; a retrospective purchase order is not automatic authorization. Approved invoices use source-15. This document does not list approved suppliers or guarantee a review completion time.
