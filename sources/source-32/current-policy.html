Nimbus sandbox environments and test data

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: Developer Experience.

Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

Applicability

Nimbus customer sandbox workspaces; production customer data is not approved sandbox test data.

Environment separation

Sandbox and production use separate keys, workspace IDs, and webhook endpoints. Sandbox orders never initiate real payment settlement. Responses include environment=sandbox. Applications must explicitly choose environment rather than infer it from an order ID.

Test-data lifecycle

Sandbox workspaces reset every Sunday at 02:00 UTC. Reset removes test orders and generated export files; workspace settings and registered webhook endpoints remain. Administrators may request one reset deferral per calendar quarter, approved before Friday 16:00 UTC.

Limits and fixture design

Sandbox keys have the ten-requests-per-minute per-operation cap in source-05, regardless of plan. Use synthetic names and reserved .invalid email addresses. Do not copy production customer records into a sandbox. Fault fixtures can simulate a timeout or duplicate event without proving a production defect.

Boundaries

Sandbox behavior is suitable for integration rehearsal but does not establish production uptime or payment settlement timing. API key expiry follows source-30. A reset deferral preserves test records only until the next scheduled reset unless a separate approved notice states otherwise.
