# Nimbus departures and equipment return

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: People Operations and IT.
Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

## Applicability
Employees leaving Nimbus; involuntary or urgent security departures require a coordinated restricted case.

## Normal departure sequence
People Operations records the final working day and access-stop timestamp in a LEAVE ticket. The manager names the replacement owner for every active service, contract, and shared folder at least three business days before departure. Do not transfer a departing person's password.

## Access and equipment
IT disables interactive access at the recorded timestamp. Service identities follow source-12 ownership transfer; disabling a human account must not silently orphan a production service. Return equipment within five business days after the final working day using the tracked Nimbus shipping label.

## Data handover
Business records stay in approved repositories with an accountable owner. Personal files may be removed only through the documented IT review process. Retention or legal-hold requirements override routine deletion; see source-18 and source-20.

## Exceptions
For an urgent departure, Security can direct immediate account suspension while People Operations finalizes the case. A courier delay is recorded with tracking evidence and a revised collection date. This policy does not authorize payroll deductions for equipment or define final-pay law.
