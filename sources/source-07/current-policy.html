Joining Nimbus: the first ten working days

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: People Operations.

Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

Applicability

Employees joining Nimbus Labs; contractors use the separate access process in source-12.

Before arrival

People Operations opens a JOIN ticket after the signed agreement is recorded. The manager supplies a role profile, working location, and equipment address at least five business days before the start date. IT provisions an individual account; shared starter passwords are prohibited.

Dependencies and exceptions

Production access is not part of the starter bundle. It follows source-12 after security orientation. A shipping delay permits a managed loan device; a personal laptop is not an automatic substitute. The manager assigns a buddy for the first ten working days.

Completion and ownership

People Operations closes JOIN only when the manager confirms each milestone or records an owner and due date for a blocker. An account activation timestamp alone does not establish onboarding completion. This guide does not define probation length or parental leave.
