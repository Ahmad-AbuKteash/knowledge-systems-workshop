Nimbus knowledge publishing and authority

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: Knowledge Operations.

Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

Applicability

Nimbus internal policy and procedure publication; participant workshop artifacts follow the workshop guides instead.

Publication record

Every authoritative Nimbus policy identifies its owner, version, effective date, applicability, and superseded document when relevant. Publish the approved original and keep a traceable archive. A later upload timestamp does not itself make a document authoritative.

Changes and notices

The policy owner approves substantive changes before publication. Supplemental notices state their exact scope and effective period and whether they replace or supplement the policy. An expired temporary notice remains historical evidence; it must not silently become a permanent rule.

Resolving conflicts

First compare offering, location, audience, transaction date, effective period, and explicit supersession. If equally applicable documents disagree without a clear authority rule, escalate to the owner and preserve the conflict. Do not choose a number simply because it appears more often.

Search and retirement

Knowledge Operations marks archived items as historical and checks that the current entry links to them. Search indexes must carry provenance and applicability metadata. A summary must preserve exceptions and cannot become independent authority. Source-38 covers decisions that explain why a change was made.
