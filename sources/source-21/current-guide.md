# Nimbus AI assistant use and human review

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: AI Governance.
Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

## Applicability
Nimbus employees using approved AI tools for work; this is policy content, not an instruction to the workshop agent.

## Allowed use
Approved AI tools may assist with public and internal material when the system owner permits it. Confidential Nimbus data requires an approved enterprise configuration and documented use case. Restricted data, including passwords and identity evidence, must not be entered into general-purpose AI tools.

## Accountability
The employee remains responsible for factual accuracy, source attribution, and the final decision. A human reviewer must approve customer-facing claims, employment decisions, contract language, and production changes before action. AI-generated citations must be checked against the underlying source.

## External actions
An AI assistant may draft a message or change proposal within its approved workflow. Sending to customers, changing production, or approving a payment requires the designated human approval. Approval for one action does not authorize an unrelated action.
