Nimbus security event reporting and triage

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: Security Operations.

Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

Applicability

Suspected security events involving Nimbus systems, devices, or information; confirmed service incidents may also invoke source-24.

Reporting

Report suspected credential exposure, unauthorized access, suspicious data transfer, or a lost managed device within one hour of discovery. Use the approved security reporting channel or its documented phone fallback. Do not wait to determine severity or prove exploitation.

Preserve evidence

Record discovery time in UTC, affected system, reporter contact, and relevant ticket or log reference. Preserve original messages and logs through approved storage. Do not post secrets or full customer exports in a general chat channel. Security coordinates containment and evidence handling.

Triage targets

Assigned security severityInitial triage target
Critical: active compromise suspected15 minutes after report
High: credible exposure without observed activity1 hour after report
Medium: suspicious event needing investigation1 business day after report

Boundaries

Security assigns severity; reporters supply observations. A triage target is not a promise of resolution or notification to customers. Privacy Operations and Legal determine external notification obligations. Source-13 governs device baseline; source-30 governs planned key rotation.
