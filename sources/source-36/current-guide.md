# Nimbus accessible documents and events

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: Workplace Accessibility.
Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

## Applicability
Nimbus internal shared documents, training, and company-organized events; individual arrangements are handled privately.

## Documents
Use semantic headings, descriptive link text, readable contrast, and table headers. Provide text alternatives for informative images. Do not place essential instructions only in a screenshot. A scanned PDF requires readable text or an equivalent accessible version.

## Meetings and events
Enable live captions for company training and provide materials at least two business days before a scheduled session. Ask about access needs through a private channel. Venue organizers check step-free access and an accessible restroom before confirming a booking.

## Exceptions and remediation
For an urgent session where advance materials are impossible, the organizer records the reason, supplies an accessible summary within one business day, and agrees an alternative with affected attendees. Urgency does not remove the need for an accessible participation option.

## Ownership and boundaries
The document owner fixes reported barriers or supplies a usable alternative within five business days. This is Nimbus's operating target, not a certification claim or a statement of legal compliance.
