# Return handover checklist

Record version: 1.0. Owner: People Operations. Access scope: workshop-public.
Record type: checklist.

Published: 2026-10-09. Effective: 2026-10-09 onward.
Before a Nimbus employee returns, the manager confirms role priorities, identifies training missed during leave, and schedules a first-week check-in. IT revalidates access rather than copying a colleague's permissions. Record an agreed 80% schedule in the personnel case and publish only availability in the team calendar. The checklist does not create a different leave entitlement. If local entitlement reconciliation is pending, People Operations resolves that issue before the manager promises a final schedule.
