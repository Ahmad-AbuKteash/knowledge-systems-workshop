# Nimbus managed devices and loan equipment

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: IT Operations.
Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

## Applicability
Company laptops and loan devices used for Nimbus work; specialized lab devices require a separate exception.

## Baseline
Nimbus laptops must have full-disk encryption, managed endpoint protection, automatic screen lock after five minutes, and supported operating-system versions. IT records the asset ID, assigned person, and compliance state. Employees may not disable management agents to improve performance.

## Replacement and loans
Standard laptop replacement is available after 36 months, subject to IT assessment. A fault that prevents work can qualify earlier. A loan device may be issued for up to 14 calendar days; IT must approve and record any extension before expiry.

## Loss and repairs
Report a lost or stolen device to IT and Security within one hour of discovery. Provide the asset ID and last known location; do not delay reporting while searching. Security coordinates account protection and remote actions. Approved repair partners receive only the minimum access needed.

## Scope and exceptions
Personal devices cannot store Nimbus customer data under this baseline. An exception requires Security's written scope and expiry; manager permission alone is insufficient. Offboarding returns follow source-11. This policy does not define mobile-device reimbursement or insurance coverage.
