# Nimbus privacy request intake and verification

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: Privacy Operations.
Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

## Applicability
Requests about personal data processed by Nimbus; contractual workspace exports use source-34.

## Intake
Privacy Operations records receipt time, requested action, requester contact, affected Nimbus service, and case ID. Acknowledge within three business days. The Nimbus operational target is completion within 30 calendar days from receipt; Privacy Operations assesses applicable legal deadlines separately.

## Verification
Use proportionate identity checks before disclosing, changing, or deleting personal data. Prefer a verified signed-in account or a challenge through the existing verified contact channel. Do not request a passport by default. Representatives must demonstrate authority to act.

## Search and action
Identify systems and data-controller responsibilities before assigning work. For customer-controlled workspace data, route to the customer's authorized administrator while recording Nimbus's role. Deletion requests must be checked for retention obligations and legal holds under source-18.

## Exceptions and communication
Escalate uncertain identity, conflicting instructions, or an approaching deadline to the privacy lead. Record any lawful extension decision and communicate its basis; do not silently reset the clock when verification begins.
