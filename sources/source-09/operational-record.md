# Exam voucher expiry and cancelled courses

Record version: 1.0. Owner: Learning and Development. Access scope: workshop-public.
Record type: notice.

Published: 2026-10-08. Effective: 2026-10-08 onward.
A Nimbus employee purchasing a certification voucher must record its expiry date in the learning request. Reimbursement does not extend the vendor's validity period. If a provider cancels a course, attach the cancellation and refund evidence to the original claim; Finance restores the refunded amount to the same year's course balance once the refund settles. An unused voucher does not transfer budget into the next year. Manager approval for the original purchase does not automatically approve a replacement booking at a higher price.
