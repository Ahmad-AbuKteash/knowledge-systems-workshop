Nimbus learning funds and certification time

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: Learning and Development.

Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

Applicability

Nimbus full-time employees; part-time monetary limits are prorated by contracted full-time fraction.

Separate budgets

The course budget and conference budget are separate. A single invoice must be allocated once to its actual category. The conference rule in source-01 remains applicable; these allowances do not create a second USD 500 conference entitlement. Business travel uses source-04 and requires its own approval.

Eligibility and reimbursement

The employee must be employed on the course start date. Submit the paid invoice and approval within 30 calendar days after purchase. A failed exam remains eligible if it was approved; a second attempt requires fresh approval and uses the remaining annual balance.

Exceptions

Mandatory security training is centrally funded and does not reduce these allowances. Unused funds and study days expire at year end. This policy does not promise promotion, salary changes, or repayment obligations after departure.
