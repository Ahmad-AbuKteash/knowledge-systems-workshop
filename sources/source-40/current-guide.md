# Nimbus sustainability and equipment reuse

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: Workplace Sustainability.
Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

## Applicability
Nimbus operational sustainability records and equipment reuse; this source establishes no verified company emissions total.

## Equipment hierarchy
Prefer repair, then internal reuse, then approved refurbishment, and finally certified recycling when an IT assessment determines a device is unsuitable for continued use. The replacement assessment in source-13 does not automatically require disposal after 36 months.

## Data safeguards
IT must complete approved data sanitization and record the result before a device leaves Nimbus custody for reuse or recycling. A factory-reset claim from the receiving organization is insufficient evidence by itself. Restricted or held records follow source-18 before sanitization.

## Measurement boundaries
Track device counts by outcome and reporting period. Report electricity in kWh separately from estimated greenhouse-gas emissions. An emissions estimate must state the activity data, factor source and year, geography, and organizational boundary. Avoid double counting a reused device as both recycled and donated.

## Claims and review
Workplace Sustainability reviews external environmental claims with the communications owner. Estimates are not audited results. This policy sets no carbon-neutral certification, emissions-reduction percentage, or automatic right to donate assigned equipment. Offboarding collection is governed by source-11.
