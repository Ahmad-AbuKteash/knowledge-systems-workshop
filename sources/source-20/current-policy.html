Nimbus contract signatures and legal review

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: Legal Operations.

Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

Applicability

Contracts signed on behalf of Nimbus Labs; purchase budget approval follows source-14.

Signature authority

Total contract commitmentAuthorized signatory
Up to and including USD 25,000Finance director
Above USD 25,000Chief operating officer

Review requirements

Legal must review non-standard terms before signature, regardless of value. A supplier processing customer personal data requires the data-processing agreement and Security review described in source-14. Calculate commitment over the full non-cancellable term, including mandatory fees, before tax.

Execution and repository

Legal Operations assigns a contract ID. The authorized signatory signs the approved version; substantive changes after approval require re-review. Store the fully executed agreement, approval record, renewal date, and termination-notice period in the contract repository within two business days.

Authority boundaries

A manager can approve business need without having signature authority. A purchase order or supplier click-through can create a commitment and must follow this process. Legal holds are issued and released in writing under source-18. This document does not enumerate all officers or provide a general power of attorney.
