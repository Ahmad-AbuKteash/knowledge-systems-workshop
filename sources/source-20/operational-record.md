# Renewal reminder does not equal termination

Record version: 1.0. Owner: Legal Operations. Access scope: workshop-public.
Record type: notice.

Published: 2026-10-07. Effective: 2026-10-07 onward.
Nimbus contract owners must review renewal reminders against the signed agreement's actual notice period. A repository reminder 30 days before renewal does not create a 30-day termination right. When requesting cancellation, attach the clause, required delivery method, deadline, and proof of receipt. Legal Operations checks disputed notice interpretation. Procurement may stop a pending purchase request but cannot thereby terminate an executed supplier agreement. Keep the termination evidence with the contract ID and preserve it if a legal hold applies.
