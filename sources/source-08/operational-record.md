# Calendar labels for collaboration days

Record version: 1.0. Owner: People Operations. Access scope: workshop-public.
Record type: email.

Published: 2026-10-05. Effective: 2026-10-05 onward.
Use the Nimbus calendar label COLLAB for planned team collaboration days. A COLLAB entry describes the agreed team pattern; it is not evidence of country approval. A person working remotely under an accessibility arrangement should appear as available without the reason for that arrangement. Calendar administrators may correct labels but cannot approve cross-country work. Requests spanning December and January must state the working days in each calendar year separately; unused allowance does not carry over.
