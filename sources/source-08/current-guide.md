# Nimbus flexible work and collaboration hours

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: People Operations.
Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

## Applicability
Nimbus employees in an approved working country; this policy does not authorize immigration or tax arrangements.

## Working pattern
Teams schedule two collaboration days each week. The manager records the chosen days in the team calendar one month ahead. Collaboration days may be remote when the whole team has an approved remote arrangement.

## Availability
Shared collaboration hours are 10:00-14:00 in the employee's contractual office time zone, Monday through Thursday. This is an availability window, not a four-hour working day. Friday meetings require participants to agree in advance.

## Working elsewhere
An employee may request up to 20 working days per calendar year from another already approved Nimbus working country. Submit the country, dates, and emergency contact at least ten business days ahead. People Operations and Security must approve before travel. Manager approval alone is insufficient.

## Exceptions and records
A documented accessibility arrangement may replace the collaboration pattern. People Operations records the applicable arrangement without publishing personal details in team calendars. Days spent on approved business travel do not consume the 20-day allowance. Expense reimbursement is governed by source-04, not this policy. Approved-country lists must be obtained from People Operations; this source does not enumerate them.
