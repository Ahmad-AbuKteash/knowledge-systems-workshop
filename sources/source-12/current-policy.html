Nimbus identity and privileged access

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: Identity Engineering.

Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

Applicability

Nimbus workforce accounts, contractors, and service identities; customer end-user roles are covered by source-33.

Access classes

Access classApproversMaximum grant duration
Standard workforce applicationManager and application ownerUntil role change or departure
Contractor accountNimbus sponsor and application owner90 calendar days
Production administratorService owner and Security8 hours

Authentication and renewal

All workforce accounts require MFA. Production administrators use phishing-resistant authentication and a ticketed purpose. Contractor access expires automatically and requires a new sponsor review for renewal. Service identities need a named human owner, a documented purpose, and quarterly review; a service identity cannot be used for interactive employee login.

Emergency access

An incident commander may request a one-hour emergency grant. Security approval and logging remain mandatory; review the grant by the next business day. Emergency access does not become an eight-hour standing grant.

Review and boundaries

Application owners review standard access quarterly. Remove access when the role no longer needs it, even if the person remains employed. Joining and leaving workflows are in source-07 and source-11. This source contains no recovery codes or usable credentials.
