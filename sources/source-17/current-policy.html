Nimbus customer incident communications

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: Customer Communications.

Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

Applicability

External updates about Nimbus service incidents; internal technical response follows source-24.

Who publishes

The incident commander supplies verified impact and recovery facts. The communications lead approves public wording. Only the designated publisher posts to the Nimbus status page. Support agents may link published updates but must not invent a recovery estimate.

Cadence

Incident classFirst public update targetUpdate interval while active
SEV1Within 20 minutes of declarationAt most 30 minutes
SEV2Within 60 minutes of declarationAt most 60 minutes

Content

State affected services, observed start time if known, customer-visible symptoms, and the next update time in UTC. Say "investigating" when cause is unconfirmed. A workaround must identify its scope and limits. Do not publish customer names, raw logs, or security-sensitive details.

Recovery

Publish monitoring status after mitigation and resolution after the commander confirms normal service. A missed update requires a prompt factual correction, not backdating. Incident classes differ from support ticket priorities in source-03; a P1 ticket alone does not declare a SEV1 incident. This guide sets no uptime-credit entitlement.
