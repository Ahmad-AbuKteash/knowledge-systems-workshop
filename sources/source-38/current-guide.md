# Nimbus architecture decisions and exceptions

Version: 2026.2. Effective date: 2026-09-01. Owner: Architecture Council.
Access scope: workshop-public. Supersedes previous-policy.md.

## Applicability
Material Nimbus technical architecture choices and time-limited exceptions; release authorization remains separate.

## Decision record
Use an ADR with an ID, owner, status, decision date, context, considered options, consequences, and links to evidence. Status is proposed, accepted, superseded, or rejected. A proposed ADR describes an option under discussion and does not authorize implementation.

## Acceptance and replacement
The accountable service owner and Architecture Council representative approve accepted decisions. Record the accepted version and date. To reverse a decision, create a new ADR that explicitly supersedes the earlier record; do not rewrite history to hide the original tradeoff.

## Exceptions
An architecture exception records the specific system, risk owner, compensating controls, and expiry no more than 90 calendar days after approval. Renewals require fresh review before expiry. An exception does not waive Security, privacy, access, or release approvals outside its stated scope.

## Follow-up
Review an accepted ADR when its recorded assumptions materially change. Benchmark numbers must identify measurement conditions; estimates must be labeled. This policy does not select a universal database, cloud, or programming language for Nimbus.
