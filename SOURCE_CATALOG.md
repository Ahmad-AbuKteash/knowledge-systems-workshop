# Nimbus source catalog

40 original synthetic source collections for **Engineering Knowledge Systems for AI Agents**. All organizations, people, policies, systems, and case records are fictional. The first six collections are retained and normalized to Nimbus; source-07 through source-40 extend the company knowledge environment.

## Using the collection

Assign one source folder to each participant or pair. Source folders contain original input documents only. Participants follow the eleven workshop steps in their own workspace; this expansion does not include completed extraction, embeddings, submissions, or answer keys.

The catalog is a navigation aid, not a replacement for source evidence. Read applicability, effective dates, document status, and exceptions inside each source. All training inputs have workshop-public access; fictional information classifications do not change actual workshop access. Business-day calculations require an applicable working calendar, which is not supplied globally. Ask for that context rather than inventing holidays or office schedules.

## Company and document boundaries

Nimbus Labs operates Nimbus Core, a self-service subscription, and Nimbus Managed Services, a separately contracted managed offering. Nimbus has a shared order API and workforce operations. This fictional framing connects the source collections without claiming a real company exists.

- Core first-payment refunds use source-03. Managed Services refunds use source-06. Source-16 explains the offering distinction. Service credits in source-26 are conditional contractual invoice credits, not the same thing as cash refunds.
- The source-01 travel meal excerpt applies through August 2026. Source-04 governs travel from September 2026; the source-01 conference registration rule remains applicable and is not a second budget in source-09.
- The source-02 limits guide applies from February through August 2026. Source-05 governs operation limits and retries from September 2026. The source-02 webhook delivery guide remains applicable alongside source-29 signature and replay rules.
- API exports in source-31 and managed workspace-exit exports in source-34 are different workflows with different download windows. Backups in source-27 are a separate recovery mechanism.
- A support ticket priority, operational incident severity, internal service objective, and contractual SLA are distinct concepts. Sources 03, 17, 24, 25, and 26 identify their boundaries.
- Proposed decisions, case records, logs, exercises, and measurements preserve observations in context. They do not silently amend company policy or establish current service health.

## Composition and provenance

There are 120 source input files: three per collection. Formats include Markdown, HTML, PDF, and PNG. The original PNG notices retain their distinct supplemental content. New operational records include emails, checklists, incident timelines, measured exercise records, a scoped hold notice, a proposed ADR, and an equipment ledger.

All new material is original synthetic workshop writing. No external company policies or web text were copied. Dates, rules, measurements, and identifiers in the records are authored fictional scenario inputs, not claims about actual operations. No real credentials, customer data, or legal entitlements are supplied.

Current policies in the new collections use version 2026.2 effective 2026-09-01; their historical excerpts are version 2025.1 effective 2025-01-01. Supplemental records carry their own publication dates and, where applicable, effective periods. A future or expired notice must be evaluated against the question date.
